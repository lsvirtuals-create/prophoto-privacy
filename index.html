This policy applies to people who use the ProPhoto AI Android app, wherever they are. It describes what the app actually does. The publisher does not run a ProPhoto server, account system, ad network, or analytics service.

What the app does

ProPhoto AI makes a headshot from one to four photos you choose on your device. You pick a style (Corporate, Studio, Creative, or Outdoor) and a provider. The default provider is xAI Grok Imagine (grok-imagine-image-2.0). You can switch to Google Gemini (gemini-nano-banana-2.1), Replicate FLUX Kontext Pro (black-forest-labs/flux-kontext-pro), or fal.ai FLUX Kontext.

You paste that provider’s API key in Settings. The app sends your photos and the style prompt to the provider you selected, using your key. You pay that provider under their own pricing. The publisher is not billed and does not receive the payment.

Information the app handles

Photos

Photos stay on your device until you tap Generate. The app then resizes each photo on the device so the long edge is at most 1024 pixels and encodes it as JPEG. It sends those images, and the style prompt, only to the provider you selected:

  • xAI: https://api.x.ai/v1/images/edits
  • Google Gemini: https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/interactions
  • Replicate: https://api.replicate.com/v1/files and the FLUX Kontext Pro predictions endpoint. Replicate’s model accepts one reference image, so only the first photo is uploaded.
  • fal.ai: https://fal.run/fal-ai/flux-pro/kontext for one photo, or https://fal.run/fal-ai/flux-pro/kontext/max/multi for two to four photos

The app does not send the photos to Lyudmila Simeonova EOOD, or to any other host.

API keys

Each key is sent only to the matching provider, as the credential for that request (Authorization: Bearer for xAI and Replicate, Authorization: Key for fal.ai, and x-goog-api-key for Gemini). The publisher never receives the key.

On Android, the app stores the key with expo-secure-store (SecureStore.setItemAsync, getItemAsync, and deleteItemAsync in lib/key-store.ts). That module encrypts the value and keeps it in the app’s private storage. The encryption key is held in the Android Keystore. The app does not turn on biometric unlock for this item, so the library default applies. The stored names are prophoto.key.xai, prophoto.key.gemini, prophoto.key.replicate, and prophoto.key.fal.

The same source file has a web path used only when the project is run in a browser. On web, the key is kept in a JavaScript memory map for that tab and is not written to localStorage. The Google Play app is the Android build, which uses the Keystore path above.

Choices and generated images

The selected provider id is saved on the device with AsyncStorage, under the name prophoto-settings. That record does not include the API key.

Generated headshots and a short history are saved on the device. The history list is AsyncStorage key prophoto.history.v1 (id, time, preset, provider id, file location, and whether a result was an illustrative demo). On Android, the image file is written under the app’s document directory at headshots/<id>.jpg. The app keeps at most 24 history items. When a newer result pushes an older one out of that list, the app deletes that older file from the document directory.

If you save a result, the app asks for permission and writes it to your photo library through Android’s media library. If you share a result, the app opens the Android share sheet. Where it goes after that is up to the app you pick.

What the publisher does not collect

The publisher does not receive your photos, your API key, your name, your email (unless you email us), your phone number, your contacts, your location, or a device identifier. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no profile. It does not include analytics, advertising, or a crash-reporting SDK. It does not record audio. The publisher does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The publisher does not run targeted ads and does not profile you.

Security

Generation requests use HTTPS. On Android the API key is encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore and kept in app-private storage. Gallery files sit in the app’s private document directory until you delete them or save them to the photo library. No independent security audit has been done, and this policy does not claim one. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

How long data stays

There is no ProPhoto server copy.

On the device:

  • An API key remains in secure storage until you clear that field and tap Save, or until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • A gallery item remains until you delete it in the Gallery tab, until it falls out of the 24-item history, or until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • A photo you saved to the Android photo library remains there until you delete it with your gallery app. Uninstalling ProPhoto does not remove those saved copies.
  • The provider choice remains until you change it, uninstall, or clear the app’s data.
  • Email you send us is kept only as long as needed to answer you and to meet any legal record-keeping duty.

A provider’s retention of a request is that provider’s policy.

Deletion

In Gallery, “Delete from gallery” removes that history entry and deletes the image file when it is inside the app’s document directory.

In Settings, clearing the key field and tapping Save calls SecureStore.deleteItemAsync for that provider.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes its private storage, including secure-store entries, the history list, and headshot files in the app document directory. It does not remove pictures you already saved to the photo library, and it does not remove anything a provider stored.

To ask about this policy, email lsvirtuals@gmail.com. The publisher has no account to close and no server copy to erase.
