This policy applies to people who use the ProPhoto AI Headshots Android app, wherever they are. It describes what the app actually does. The app has no ads, no analytics, and no advertising ID. The publisher does not sell personal information.

What the app does

ProPhoto AI Headshots makes a headshot from one to four photos you choose on your device. You pick a style (Corporate, Studio, Creative, or Outdoor). The app is for people aged 18 and older.

The default mode is ProPhoto credits. When you tap Generate, the app sends your photos and the style prompt over HTTPS to the ProPhoto backend. The backend forwards them to xAI to generate the headshot, using xAI Grok Imagine (grok-imagine-image) and a key that stays on the server. The photos are processed only for that request. They are held in memory on our server and are not written to disk or a database. They are not kept after the request ends. xAI then handles the request under its own terms and privacy documents, which are linked below. We do not control how long xAI keeps a copy, or where it stores it.

Credits are sold as one-time Google Play purchases. There is no subscription. Google processes the payment. We do not receive your card details. We keep a purchase record and a credit balance so we can provide the credits you bought and so the same purchase cannot be credited twice.

Sign-in is optional. If you choose to sign in, you use your email address and a one-time link, or a short code from the same email. There is no password. We use the email for account and credit management, for example to restore your balance. You can also use credits on a device without signing in with email.

Settings → Advanced is an optional own-API-key mode. You can paste a key for xAI Grok Imagine (grok-imagine-image-2.0), Google Gemini (gemini-nano-banana-2.1), Replicate FLUX Kontext Pro (black-forest-labs/flux-kontext-pro), or fal.ai FLUX Kontext. In that mode the keys stay on your device, encrypted with the Android Keystore, and your photos go directly from the device to the provider you chose, not through the ProPhoto backend. You pay that provider under their own pricing. The publisher is not billed and does not receive the payment or the key.

Information the app handles

Photos

Photos stay on your device until you tap Generate. The app then resizes each photo on the device so the long edge is at most 1024 pixels and encodes it as JPEG.

In ProPhoto credits mode, those images and the style prompt are sent over HTTPS to the ProPhoto backend, which forwards them to xAI at https://api.x.ai/v1/images/edits. The backend does not store the photos. After the request ends, they are discarded from our server. What xAI keeps, and for how long, is governed by xAI’s own documents, linked in the transfers section below.

In own-API-key mode the app sends the images and the style prompt from your device straight to the provider you selected, and not to the ProPhoto backend:

  • xAI: https://api.x.ai/v1/images/edits
  • Google Gemini: https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/interactions
  • Replicate: https://api.replicate.com/v1/files and the FLUX Kontext Pro predictions endpoint. Replicate’s model accepts one reference image, so only the first photo is uploaded.
  • fal.ai: https://fal.run/fal-ai/flux-pro/kontext for one photo, or https://fal.run/fal-ai/flux-pro/kontext/max/multi for two to four photos

Credits, purchases, and accounts

To provide credits, the backend stores a random account token as a hash, a credit balance, and a purchase record. The purchase record includes the Google Play purchase token, the product id, and the credits granted, so a purchase cannot be credited twice. Google Play processes the payment. We do not receive card numbers or other payment-card details.

The Android app can create this credit account with a device token and no email. If a generation fails after a credit was used, the credit is returned to the balance.

Optional email sign-in

If you sign in, we store the email address you provide and use it for account and credit management. The app sends a one-time link and a short code to that address. They expire after a short time, and there is no password. The sign-in email is sent through our email delivery provider (Resend, when that service is configured). We do not sell the email address.

Report this result

Each generated result includes a “Report this result” control. If you use it, the app sends a report to the ProPhoto backend. A report includes the result identifier, the reason you picked (for example, that the image does not look like the person, is inappropriate or unsafe, is poor quality, or something else), and an optional short note you type. It does not include your original photos or the generated image. If you have a credit account on that device, the report may be stored with that account. If the report cannot be sent immediately, the app can keep it on the device until it can be sent.

API keys

This section applies only if you use own-API-key mode. Each key is sent only to the matching provider, as the credential for that request (Authorization: Bearer for xAI and Replicate, Authorization: Key for fal.ai, and x-goog-api-key for Gemini). The publisher never receives the key.

On Android, the app stores the key with expo-secure-store in the app’s private storage. The value is encrypted, and the encryption key is held in the Android Keystore. The app does not turn on biometric unlock for this item, so the library default applies.

The same storage module has a web path used only when the project is run in a browser. On web, the key is kept in a JavaScript memory map for that tab and is not written to localStorage. The Google Play app is the Android build, which uses the Keystore path above.

Choices and generated images

Your provider choice is saved on the device. That record does not include the API key.

Generated headshots and a short history are saved on the device (when the result was made, which style and provider were used, where the file is, and whether a result was an illustrative demo). On Android, the image file is written under the app’s document directory. The app keeps at most 24 history items. When a newer result pushes an older one out of that list, the app deletes that older file from the document directory.

If you save a result, the app asks for permission and writes it to your photo library through Android’s media library. If you share a result, the app opens the Android share sheet. Where it goes after that is up to the app you pick.

What the publisher does not collect

The publisher does not receive your API key or your card details. It does not ask for your name, your phone number, your contacts, or your location, and it does not record an advertising ID. The app does not include analytics, advertising, or a crash-reporting SDK. It does not record audio. The publisher does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The publisher does not run targeted ads and does not profile you.

Website checkout

The same credits backend may also be used on the ProPhoto website. Website sign-in uses your email and a one-time link, as described above. Card or Revolut Pay on the website is a one-time purchase handled by Revolut. The publisher does not receive the card number. Revolut’s privacy notice is https://www.revolut.com/legal/privacy/. The Android app does not offer that checkout. Packs bought in the Android app are paid through Google Play.

Those companies are based in the United States, and they and their subprocessors may process the data in the United States or in other countries. xAI is one example of a US provider. It is used in credits mode, and it can also be chosen in own-API-key mode.

Security

Generation requests use HTTPS. On Android an API key you enter is encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore and kept in app-private storage. Gallery files sit in the app’s private document directory until you delete them or save them to the photo library. Photos sent in credits mode are not stored on our server. No independent security audit has been done, and this policy does not claim one. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

How long data stays

The ProPhoto backend does not keep photos.

On the server, in credits mode:

  • The account token hash, email if you signed in, credit balance, and purchase record stay so a purchase cannot be credited twice and so the balance can be provided or restored.
  • A report you submit stays so we can review it.
  • A one-time sign-in link or code is kept only until it expires or is used.
  • A photo is not written to disk or a database.

On the device:

  • An API key remains in secure storage until you clear that field and tap Save, or until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • A gallery item remains until you delete it in the Gallery tab, until it falls out of the 24-item history, or until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • A photo you saved to the Android photo library remains there until you delete it with your gallery app. Uninstalling ProPhoto does not remove those saved copies.
  • The provider choice remains until you change it, uninstall, or clear the app’s data.
  • A report that could not be sent may remain on the device until it is sent, or until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Email you send us is kept only as long as needed to answer you and to meet any legal record-keeping duty.

A provider’s retention of a request is that provider’s policy.

Delete your account and data

To delete your ProPhoto account and the data we hold for it, email lsvirtuals@gmail.com and ask us to delete your account and data. Please include the email address you used to sign in, if you signed in. We will delete the credit account, email address, credit balance, purchase records, and reports we hold for that account. This does not erase a photo from our server, because the server does not keep photos. It does not close a Google Play order, and it does not remove copies a provider stored or pictures you already saved to your photo library.

You can also delete data on the device yourself. In Gallery, “Delete from gallery” removes that history entry and deletes the image file when it is inside the app’s document directory. In Settings, clearing an API key field and tapping Save removes that key from secure storage.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes its private storage, including saved keys, the history list, queued reports, and headshot files in the app document directory. It does not remove pictures you already saved to the photo library, it does not remove the server credit account, and it does not remove anything a provider stored. To delete the server account after you uninstall, email us as described above.
